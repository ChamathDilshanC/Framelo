# Emerald assets

The finance dashboard, market rows, ambient glow, separator rule, call-to-action
container and pill, and the Verdant monogram are original SVG artwork created
for Framelo. They contain no embedded reference image, third-party photograph,
screenshot, or copied logo.

"Verdant" is an invented placeholder brand, as are the ticker symbols, holdings
and figures shown on the screen. Nothing here names or depicts a real company,
product or financial instrument.

`finance.svg` is replaceable screen artwork: it is what the device shows until a
PNG, JPG, WebP, MP4 or WebM is assigned through Screen media. iPhone model
attribution remains in `public/devices/CREDITS.md`.
