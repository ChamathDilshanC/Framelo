# Reference poster collection

Four editable compositions recreated from the four reference images supplied by the user:
Creative Agency, Digital Stress, Sales Person, and Bestrade Portfolio.
The names and copy follow those references; the compositions do not claim endorsement by the depicted brands.

Poster ornaments and website artwork are local SVGs generated by
`scripts/generate-reference-artwork.mjs`. Devices use the existing Framelo models
and retain their existing model credits. Headlines are native editable text.

`hand.png` is an original generated transparent photograph, made with the built-in
ImageGen tool (not the CLI) on 2026-09-26, saved in this directory for portable
preview, project persistence and export.

Generation prompt:

> Create a photorealistic advertising asset, a single realistic human right hand and forearm with warm medium tan skin, dramatically lit by vivid orange studio rim lighting from the left and neutral soft light from the right. TRUE TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND, isolated cutout, no text, no objects, NO PHONE. The hand is posed as if holding a large smartphone diagonally, with fingertips wrapping around its absent left edge and the thumb visible on the right. Palm faces camera, relaxed cupping grip. Wrist enters from bottom right corner, palm occupies lower right, fingers curve up the left. Imaginary phone runs from upper left to lower right at 27 degrees clockwise from vertical. Leave the large diagonal rectangular region over palm unobstructed for a phone to be composited later. View is close up from slightly above. Entire wrist and fingers visible except wrist cropped at bottom. Detailed natural skin creases, anatomically accurate five fingers, premium 3D product advertising photography. Portrait canvas 4:5, hand occupies lower 80%, empty transparency above and upper left. No painted or cartoon appearance, no background glow.
