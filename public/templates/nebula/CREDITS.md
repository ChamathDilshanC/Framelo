# Nebula assets

The hand silhouette, illustrated music-player portrait, ambient glow, and media
control icons are original SVG artwork created for Framelo. They contain no
embedded reference image, third-party photograph, or copied monogram.

The hand is a transparent illustration, separate from the real iPhone model.
The default player is replaceable screen artwork; its title and artist labels
follow the supplied design brief. iPhone model attribution remains in
`public/devices/CREDITS.md`.
