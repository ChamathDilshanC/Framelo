## How these files reached Framelo

The models, their per-model orientation/UV corrections and the material-tuning approach used to
render them were taken from
[Phone Mockup Studio](https://github.com/quentin-pla/phone-mockup-studio) by Quentin PLA — that
project's application code is MIT-licensed, and its own `CREDITS.md` carries the same attribution
reproduced above. Framelo integrates the assets and rendering techniques only; its editor,
timeline, animation engine and UI are its own.

## Framelo studio devices

`ipad.glb` and `macbook.glb` are original, stylized Framelo geometry generated by `scripts/generate-studio-devices.mjs`. No third-party meshes or textures. These are presentation models, not official Apple assets or dimensionally exact hardware replicas.

Keyboard legends use Helvetiker from the Three.js examples (Copyright 2004 MAGENTA Ltd). The bundled font retains its original license metadata; see `KEYBOARD-FONT-LICENSE.txt`. Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/fonts/helvetiker_regular.typeface.json

## Imported MacBook Pro 2020

`macbook-pro-2020.glb` is converted from the user-supplied `Macbook-Pro-2020/Macbook Pro 2020.obj` and accompanying JPG textures. The supplied MTL contains a single wireframe material; the importer restores surface materials, maps the keyboard and Touch Bar, converts the supplied logo masks, and adds an editable display. The original `.max` file is not required at runtime.

The supplied folder does not include an author or licence statement. This asset is not asserted to be Framelo-authored or covered by the iPhone assets' CC BY licence. Conversion script: `scripts/import-macbook-pro-2020.mjs`.
